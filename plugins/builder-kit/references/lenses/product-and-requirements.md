# Product and Requirements Lens

Ask only what materially changes the design:

- Who uses this and what decision/job becomes easier?
- What observable outcome defines success?
- What is explicitly in and out of scope?
- Which workflow is primary, and which edge cases matter now?
- What existing behavior must remain compatible?
- Which assumptions need user/product validation?
- What is the smallest coherent release that delivers value?

Turn answers into behavior and acceptance criteria. Do not invent user needs, metrics, or roadmap priority.
