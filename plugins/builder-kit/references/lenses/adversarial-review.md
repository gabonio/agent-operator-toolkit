# Adversarial Review Lens

Challenge material decisions, not wording or local style.

For each finding provide:

1. assumption or decision under challenge;
2. plausible failure/counterexample and affected users/system;
3. evidence, clearly separated from inference or hypothesis;
4. impact and likelihood;
5. cheapest useful validation, mitigation, or decision;
6. disposition: must fix, should fix, accepted risk, or user decision.

Default to three or four material findings per pass. Then report overlooked strengths, residual risks, and `ready`, `ready with accepted risks`, `revise`, or `blocked on decision`.

Do not manufacture certainty, duplicate findings, bikeshed established style, or continue when objections have low expected value. A same-context multi-lens pass is not independent review; name the review mechanism honestly. Remain read-only unless changes are separately authorized.
