# Reliability and Operations Lens

- Define only needed availability, latency, and capacity expectations.
- Cover timeouts, retries/idempotency, rate limits/backpressure, degradation, recovery, and reconciliation.
- Expose user-impacting failures through existing telemetry without leaking sensitive data.
- Plan configuration/secrets, promotion, rollout/rollback, ownership, and post-deploy evidence.

Prefer existing operational platforms and conventions. Do not add observability or infrastructure products without an approved need.
