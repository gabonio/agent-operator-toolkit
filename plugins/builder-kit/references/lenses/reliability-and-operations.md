# Reliability and Operations Lens

- Define availability/latency/capacity expectations only where the product needs them.
- Identify timeouts, retries, idempotency, rate limits, backpressure, and degradation behavior.
- Ensure logs, metrics, traces, and alerts expose user-impacting failure without leaking sensitive data.
- Plan configuration, secrets, environment promotion, feature rollout, rollback, and support ownership.
- Consider dependency outages, partial failure, restart/recovery, and data reconciliation.
- Define production-readiness and post-deploy verification evidence.

Prefer existing operational platforms and conventions. Do not add observability or infrastructure products without an approved need.
